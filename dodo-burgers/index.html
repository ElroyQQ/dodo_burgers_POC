What actually happened

When land reclamation reached the old Tuas mudflats, sonar crews picked up movement nobody could place. Descendants of Raphus cucullatus — the dodo of Mauritius — had survived by going somewhere nobody thought to check: underwater.

Species: Raphus cucullatus var. natans
