## Scope and mode

Surface: `index.html` (the whole site — single-page storefront). Mode: Persuade (visitor decides to explore the menu / build a burger / add to cart).

Audience: visitors evaluating a fictional Singapore burger restaurant; secondarily, viewers of a portfolio/demo piece. Job: browse menu, build a custom burger, understand the brand story, add to cart. Proof/content: real menu photos, the "aquatic dodo rediscovered in Singapore" backstory, Singaporean-localized copy. Constraints: real-photo-only image policy (one standing exception: the animated SVG builder-preview graphic); menu items, prices, copy, FAQ, builder mechanics, and cart behavior are unchanged — visual-only redesign.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dodo Burgers as a precision-engineered product launch — not a cute mascot site. Refuses the current friendly pastel/whimsical-card default (cream ground, rounded playful cards, soft coral CTAs) in favor of confident, engineered-product presentation. The whimsical "extinct species, precision-rediscovered" premise is told completely straight, with total visual confidence, the way a robotics company presents its flagship hardware.

OWN-WORLD: near-black navy grounds (`hsl(210 82% 4%)` family, ≈ `#020b14`) alternating with clean white/off-white content bands (`#f8fafc`/`#ffffff`), one restrained cool steel-blue accent (`hsl(210 50% 60%)`, ≈ `#6699cc`) carrying every CTA, active state, and stat highlight — no coral, no teal. Heavy geometric display headline face at weight 800 (system stack: Space Grotesk or equivalent geometric sans), clean sans body (Inter-class stack, matching what's already loaded). Fully-rounded pill buttons, thin hairline dividers, big proof-stat rows (numeral + label blocks: burgers built, ingredients, years since rediscovery, countries the dodo was "sighted" in — reusing existing real facts/menu data, never inventing commercial claims). Real photography (menu photos, hero dodo composite) re-graded cooler and darker with dramatic, moody vignette lighting — large-scale, tightly cropped so the subject dominates the frame the way a product shot does, replacing the current bright, cheerful, distant compositions. No illustration introduced anywhere except the existing standing SVG builder-preview exception.

STORY: A visitor should read Dodo Burgers as a seriously, confidently engineered product — the in-universe joke lands through composure, not cuteness. Same backstory, same menu, same builder and cart, told with total visual confidence in a colder, more dramatic register.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed near-black hero at viewport height. Thin top nav (logo left, nav links center/right, one pill "Order Now" button top-right). Bold 800-weight headline (existing hero copy, unchanged) over a re-graded, tightly-cropped, large-scale hero photo (the dodo composite regraded cooler/darker/moodier, dominating the frame rather than sitting distant in it) with a dark gradient vignette for text legibility. One-line subhead. Primary pill CTA ("Build Your Burger" / scroll-to-menu) + secondary text link with a trailing arrow, mirroring the reference's primary/secondary CTA pairing. Small quiet scroll cue at the base.

FORM: User-pinned direction (reference: engineeredarts.com — confident dark tech-product-launch world), confirmed directly by the user; no concept-seed dice roll run, since the world was pinned rather than open.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
